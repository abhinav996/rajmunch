Quick Ideas

From our kitchen to yours

Three easy ways to use Rajmunch ingredients this week.

🍫 Bestseller

Rich Hot Chocolate

  1. Warm milk with a pinch of cardamom.
  2. Whisk in 2 tbsp of Rajmunch cocoa powder and sugar.
  3. Serve hot, topped with a dusting of cocoa.
🍞 Easy

Soft Flax Bread

  1. Activate active dry yeast in warm water with sugar.
  2. Mix dough with flour, a handful of flax seeds and salt.
  3. Let rise, then bake at 190°C for 25 minutes.
🥜 Healthy

Crunchy Trail Mix

  1. Mix sunflower, pumpkin and chia seeds in a bowl.
  2. Toss with a pinch of salt and a drizzle of honey.
  3. Store in a jar — grab a handful every day.
